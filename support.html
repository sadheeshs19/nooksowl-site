Support

Last updated 30 October 2026

Need help with Nooks Owl? We're here.

Report someone

Tap Report on their topic, in the chat menu, during a call or after a call. Our team reviews every report within 24 hours. You can also block them so they can't contact you again.

Sign-in problems

Open the sign-in email on the same phone that has Nooks Owl, and use the newest email. Links expire after a while and work only once. Check your spam folder.

Delete your account

Profile → Delete account. It's permanent and removes everything linked to your account.

Contact us

Email us and we'll reply within 2 working days.
